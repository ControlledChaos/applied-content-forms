Applied Content Forms began as a fork of [Advanced Custom Fields PRO](https://www.advancedcustomfields.com/pro/) version 5.9.6 plus a fork of [Advanced Custom Fields: Extended PRO](https://www.acf-extended.com/pro) version 0.8.8.6. The long-time ACF user may recognize appearance of these pages as a nod to the old Advanced Custom Fields website.

This plugin was actually part of a boilerplate for required, site-specific plugins that I would develop for my clients' websites. It took advantage of ACF's filters for bundling with themes or other plugins. I later decided to move the ACF and ACFE files into a separate plugin.

Development of this standalone version has thus far been focused on fully merging the two plugins, and bringing a tidy, legible code format to all files. Minor feature improvements have been made but no major advancements or new features yet. The goal is to have a brand-free, drama-free plugin that does what ACF & ACFE do, and more, with one seamless user interface.

## Why Bother Doing All This Work?

One can simply install Advanced Custom Fields and Advanced Custom Fields: Extended, so what is the point of this plugin? I will begin the answer with a short story.

At the height of my occupation as a freelance WordPress website developer, I was and avid user and promoter of the Advanced Custom Fields plugin. Sometimes I would interact on Twitter with Eliot Condon, the creator of ACF.

I began using ACF when it was in version 4. Back the the plugin was was monetized by selling "add on" plugins which offered a valuable field type for $25 AUD each. Those fields were Gallery, Repeater, Flexible Content, and Options Pages.

The first I purchased was the Gallery field. Then the Repeater, then Options Pages. Somehow Eliot Condon took notice. By this time he had released ACF Pro, which included the four add-ons, and Eliot offered me a copy. So I have an unlimited, lifetime license for ACF Pro which only cost me the one time fee of $75 AUD, which came out to about $72 USD. I suppose he was looking for folks to spread the word about the new ACF Pro plugin.

### What I Liked About ACF

If Advanced Custom Fields wasn't the first custom fields plugin for WordPress, it was certainly the first to have success. But it was a grassroots success, quietly gaining popularity by offering much-needed functionality and quality code. Eliot Condon did not beleaguer us with promotion and upsells. He did not try to make the ACF user interface stand out from the native UI, which was a blessing to those of us who regularly created custom admin themes to match a frontend theme. No, ACF simply integrated seamlessly into the WP admin.

### The Sale of Advanced Custom Fields

When Eliot Condon announced that he had sold Advanced Custom Fields to Delicious Brains, distributor of various WordPress products, I suspected that the home-spun nature of ACF would be eliminated in the push for corporate branding. I was right.

The new interface of Advanced Custom Fields is obnoxious, with styles that are not consistent with the native admin pages, plus large ads for upgrading, and links to buy website hosting.

### The Theft of Advanced Custom Fields

Yes, there is no theft of code under the GPL license issued with WordPress, which is inherited by compatible products. This is what allows me to re-release ACF & ACFE. But Advanced Custom Fields had something shady pulled on them which was not right, even for its new corporate overlords.

The megalomattic czar of WordPress has issues with the new ACF interface. Not the issues I have, he has hypocritical, anti-competitive issues. Although guilty himself, he could have mandated that certain content and links be removed from the ACF interface if they wanted to continue to be included in the WordPress plugin directory. Instead he took control of the Advanced Custom Fields repository in WordPress then renamed it. He changed the plugin name and kept all the statistics & SEO acquired by ACF over the years.

This man has generated much antipathy over previous controversies, and the backlash of what he did to Advanced Custom Fields was too much for even him to withstand; so his actions were reversed. But one cannot un-ring a bell and it was time for me to personally abandon the use of WordPress.
